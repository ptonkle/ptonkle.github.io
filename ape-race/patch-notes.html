Ape Race patch notes

Play Ape Race · ptonk

v1.12.1 2026-10-15

  • Race Setup and the lobby: every map's picture now shows in a row above your ape; click one to pick it.

v1.12.0 2026-10-15

  • New map: Banana Bay Battle. Swing through a pirate battle at 3 pm, from a tall pier to a treasure island, in pirate outfits (the Floor is Lava jaguar is a navy officer).
  • Fall in the sea and you're put back on the last ship you passed. Flagged barrels mark the edges, and bouncy barrels can save you. New weather there: Squall.
  • Unlockable costumes: beat a map's Author time and wear its costume on any ape, on any map (Costume, beside Colours).
  • Sector Ape: the shortcut through the wreck is bigger and fires you through it with a speed boost.
  • Yeti Peak: half as many low vines.
  • Settings shows the weather you actually chose. Online needs everyone on this version.

v1.11.4 2026-10-15

  • Sunset Ruins: in the online lobby the temple's mouth shows between the menus and your ape, instead of hiding behind a panel.

v1.11.3 2026-10-15

  • Sunset Ruins: flying into the temple, the camera no longer ends up on top of the mouth's stone roof; it stays inside with you.
  • Sunset Ruins: no more rock poking up through the floor just inside the mouth.
  • Sunset Ruins: computer players climb back out of the river by its low vines instead of getting stuck on a bank, and the four levels are spread apart again.

v1.11.2 2026-10-15

  • Sunset Ruins: a new lobby spot: your ape stands on a ruin in the river with the temple's mouth and the sunset behind it, instead of looking at the ground.
  • Sunset Ruins: the carved wall panels sit flat on every wall, and their banana glyphs glow where you can see them.

v1.11.1 2026-10-15

  • Sunset Ruins: the temple's stone face is solid now; only its open mouth lets you in. Hit the face and you drop into the ravine.
  • Sunset Ruins: the bridge's boost vines aim a little further into the mouth.

v1.11.0 2026-10-15

  • What's New: these patch notes, on the main menu (the version number in the corner opens them too). A New marker shows after an update; tick Show minor patches to see every small fix.
